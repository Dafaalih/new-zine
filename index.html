the philosophy

built for the misfits, thinkers, poets, and punk artists who still believe in physical paper in a digital void.

how to fold your mini zine

  1. 1 lay the sheet flat, printed side up. fold it in half horizontally (top edge to bottom edge) along the dashed fold line.
  2. 2 fold in half again along the vertical spine in the middle, so the right side folds over the left.
  3. 3 fold in half a third time — this time accordion it, so the left side opens out to the left.
  4. 4 cut along the solid cut line across the middle two columns at the horizontal centre.
  5. 5 unfold completely, then refold into a little booklet: front cover (page 1) up, page 8 on the back.
